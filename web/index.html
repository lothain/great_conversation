The Great Conversation

Human ideas have evolved over time and by reacting to one another.

  • The Great Conversation is meant to help explore this process
  • Time is set with the slider along the bottom.
  • Members represent humans or books (written by humans) who have contributed.
  • Utterances represent an encapsulated idea or contribution
